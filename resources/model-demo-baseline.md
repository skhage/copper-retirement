## 7. Caveats

* **Synthetic data:** Training data is synthetic (TMForum CDM). Uniform feature distributions limit real discriminative power — pipeline patterns and MLOps workflow are production-valid, but absolute accuracy numbers are not representative of a real telco deployment.
* **Leakage fix:** V5 explicitly excludes `composite_risk_score` and `_prank` percentile-rank features that caused target leakage in V4.
* **Small slices:** 14 of 21 fairness slices have n < 50 (marked "small sample"). Results directionally valid but not statistically robust.
